FORMULA REFERENCE
Inventory Turnover
Turnover = COGS / Avg Inventory Value
Higher = faster-moving stock. Benchmark: 6–12× for retail.
Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO)
DIO = (Avg Inventory / COGS) × 365
Days stock sits before being sold. Lower is better.
GMROI — Gross Margin Return on Investment
GMROI = Gross Profit / Avg Inventory Cost
Measures profitability per ₹ of inventory held. Target ≥ 2.
Service Level / Fill Rate
Fill Rate = Orders Filled / Total Orders × 100
Percentage of orders fulfilled without backorder. Target 95–99%.
Carrying Cost Ratio
Carrying Cost % = (Total Holding Cost / Avg Inventory Value) × 100
Includes storage, insurance, obsolescence, capital cost. Benchmark: 20–30%.
Stockout Rate
Stockout Rate = Stockout Events / Total Demand Events × 100
Each % of stockout can translate to ~1–3% lost revenue.

FORECASTING MODEL METRICS
Holt-Winters (Triple ETS)
ŷ(t+h) = l(t) + h·b(t) + s(t+h-m)
Best for: seasonal goods, holiday spikes. Requires ≥2 full seasons of history.
MAPE 11.2%
ARIMA / SARIMA
ARIMA(p,d,q)(P,D,Q)m
Best for: stable trend items, slow movers. Needs stationarity. More data-hungry.
MAPE 14.1%
Moving Average
MA(n) = Σ(y(t-i)) / n
Best for: new products with <30 days history. Simple, interpretable baseline.
MAPE 18.6%

REORDER CALCULATIONS
Safety Stock
SS = Z × σ_d × √(Lead Time)
Z = service level Z-score (95% → 1.65, 99% → 2.33). σ_d = std dev of daily demand.
90% service levelZ = 1.28
95% service levelZ = 1.65
98% service levelZ = 2.05
99% service levelZ = 2.33
Reorder Point (ROP)
ROP = (Avg Daily Demand × Lead Time) + Safety Stock
The stock level that triggers a purchase order. System alerts when stock ≤ ROP.
Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)
EOQ = √( 2 × D × S / H )
D = annual demand units, S = cost per order placement, H = annual holding cost per unit. Minimises total ordering + holding cost.
Max Stock Level
Max Stock = ROP + EOQ
Upper bound for inventory. Purchasing above this creates overstock and excess carrying cost.

URGENCY CLASSIFICATION
Critical
Current stock < Safety Stock. Stockout imminent. Immediate PO required.
Warning
Current stock < ROP but ≥ Safety Stock. Order within supplier lead time.
Optimal
Current stock ≥ ROP. No action needed. Monitor for trend changes.

COSTING METHODS COMPARISON
FIFO — First In First Out
Oldest stock sold first. COGS reflects older (usually lower) prices. Ending inventory at recent cost. Best for perishables and inflation environments.
LIFO — Last In First Out
Newest stock sold first. COGS reflects recent prices, lowering taxable income in inflationary periods. Not allowed under IFRS.
Weighted Average Cost
WAC = Total Cost / Total Units
Recalculates average cost on every receipt. Smooths price volatility. Simplest for high-volume homogenous goods.
